# Backlog: <project>

Ideas live here until a milestone is closed. **This file is not a queue of work —
it is a promise that the idea will not be lost.** That promise is what makes
deferring one possible; ideas that get lost are ideas you will refuse to defer.

## Rules

1. **A new idea goes here by default**, never into `REQUIREMENTS.md`. Adding to
   the active milestone requires closing a requirement or a dated amendment.
2. **Nothing is deleted.** Entries move to Promoted or Declined, with a date.
   The record of what you chose not to build is as useful as what you built.
3. **Reviewed at milestone close only.** Reviewing it mid-flight is how the
   milestone stops closing.
